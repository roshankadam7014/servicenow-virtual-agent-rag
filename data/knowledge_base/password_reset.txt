Title: Password Reset Instructions

Problem:
An employee has forgotten their corporate password.

Instructions:
1. Open the company's approved password reset portal.
2. Follow the identity verification process.
3. Complete the password reset instructions.
4. Sign in again using the new password.

Security:
Never share passwords or verification codes with another person.
IT support must follow the company's identity verification policy.

Escalation:
If self-service reset fails, contact the authorized IT support team.

Category: Account Access
