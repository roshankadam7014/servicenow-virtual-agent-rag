# Account Lockout Troubleshooting

## Problem Description
An employee cannot sign in to their corporate account because the account has been locked after multiple unsuccessful login attempts.

## Common Symptoms
- The login page displays an account locked message.
- The employee cannot access corporate applications.
- Login attempts fail even when the correct password is entered.
- The account is temporarily blocked after repeated failed attempts.

## Possible Causes
- Multiple incorrect password attempts.
- An outdated password saved on another device.
- Background applications repeatedly attempting authentication.
- Suspicious or unusual login activity.
- Organization-defined account security restrictions.

## Troubleshooting Steps

1. Stop repeated login attempts to avoid extending the lockout.
2. Verify that you are using the correct corporate username.
3. Check whether your password was recently changed.
4. If your organization provides an approved self-service account unlock portal, follow its identity verification process.
5. Check whether other devices or applications are using an outdated saved password.
6. If the account remains locked, contact the authorized IT support team.

## When to Escalate
Contact IT support if:
- The account remains locked after following the approved unlock process.
- The employee cannot access the self-service portal.
- The account repeatedly becomes locked.
- The employee suspects unauthorized login attempts.
- Multiple employees experience the same account lockout issue.

## Security Precautions
- Never share your password or MFA verification codes.
- Never ask another person to unlock your account using their credentials.
- Report suspicious login notifications to the authorized IT security team.
- Use only organization-approved account recovery procedures.

## Expected Resolution
The employee regains access after completing the organization's approved identity verification and account unlock process. If the issue persists, IT support must investigate further.
