# VPN Connectivity Troubleshooting

## Problem Description
An employee cannot connect to the organization's corporate VPN or experiences frequent VPN disconnections.

## Common Symptoms
- VPN connection fails.
- VPN disconnects frequently.
- Unable to access internal company resources.
- VPN authentication fails.
- VPN connection becomes very slow.

## Possible Causes
- Unstable internet connection.
- Incorrect VPN credentials.
- Expired password.
- Outdated VPN client.
- Incorrect VPN configuration.
- Temporary VPN server outage.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Verify that your internet connection is working.
2. Confirm that you are using the correct corporate username and password.
3. Check whether your corporate password has expired.
4. Disconnect and reconnect to the VPN.
5. Restart the approved VPN application.
6. Check whether the VPN client requires an update.
7. Restart your computer and try connecting again.
8. If the issue continues, contact authorized IT support.

## When to Escalate
Contact IT support if:
- VPN authentication repeatedly fails.
- Multiple employees cannot connect.
- VPN disconnects continuously.
- Internal resources remain inaccessible after troubleshooting.

## Security Precautions
- Use only the organization-approved VPN client.
- Never share VPN credentials.
- Do not disable corporate security controls.
- Report suspicious VPN login activity.

## Expected Resolution
The employee establishes a stable VPN connection and can access authorized corporate resources.
