Title: VPN Connection Troubleshooting

Problem:
Employees cannot connect to the corporate VPN.

Troubleshooting steps:
1. Check whether the internet connection is working.
2. Verify that the employee is using the correct VPN address.
3. Confirm that the employee's account is active.
4. Restart the approved VPN client and try again.
5. Check the displayed VPN error message.

Escalation:
If the problem continues, contact the IT support team.
Include the error message and the time the issue occurred.

Category: Network
