# Microsoft Teams Audio and Video Troubleshooting

## Problem Description
An employee experiences audio, video, or meeting connectivity issues while using Microsoft Teams.

## Common Symptoms
- Microphone is not working.
- Other participants cannot hear the employee.
- Camera is not detected.
- Video appears blurry or freezes.
- No sound during meetings.
- Teams calls disconnect unexpectedly.
- Unable to join or connect to meetings.

## Possible Causes
- Incorrect microphone or speaker selection.
- Camera or microphone permissions are disabled.
- Another application is using the camera or microphone.
- Unstable internet connection.
- Outdated Teams application.
- Outdated audio or camera drivers.
- Incorrect device settings.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Verify that your internet connection is stable.
2. Restart the Microsoft Teams application.
3. Open Teams device settings and select the correct microphone, speaker, and camera.
4. Check that microphone and camera permissions are enabled.
5. Close other applications that may be using the microphone or camera.
6. Test your microphone and speaker using the available device settings.
7. Check whether Microsoft Teams requires an update.
8. Restart your computer and try joining the meeting again.
9. If the issue continues, contact authorized IT support.

## When to Escalate
Contact IT support if:
- Microphone or camera is not detected.
- Audio problems continue after checking device settings.
- Teams repeatedly disconnects.
- Multiple employees experience meeting connectivity problems.
- Teams crashes or fails to start.

## Security Precautions
- Grant camera and microphone permissions only to trusted applications.
- Do not install unauthorized audio or video drivers.
- Use organization-approved communication applications.
- Report suspicious meeting invitations.

## Expected Resolution
The employee can join Microsoft Teams meetings and use microphone, speaker, and camera successfully.
