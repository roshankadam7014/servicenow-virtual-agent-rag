# Wi-Fi Connectivity Troubleshooting

## Problem Description
An employee cannot connect to the corporate Wi-Fi network or experiences unstable wireless connectivity.

## Common Symptoms
- Unable to connect to corporate Wi-Fi.
- Connected to Wi-Fi but no internet access.
- Wi-Fi disconnects frequently.
- Incorrect Wi-Fi password error.
- Slow network performance.
- Unable to access internal company applications.

## Possible Causes
- Incorrect Wi-Fi credentials.
- Weak wireless signal.
- Router or access point issues.
- Incorrect IP configuration.
- DNS resolution problems.
- Outdated wireless network drivers.
- Temporary network outage.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Confirm that Wi-Fi is enabled on your device.
2. Verify that you are connecting to the correct corporate Wi-Fi network.
3. Re-enter your approved Wi-Fi credentials.
4. Move closer to the wireless access point.
5. Disconnect from Wi-Fi and reconnect.
6. Restart your computer and try again.
7. Check whether other employees are experiencing the same issue.
8. If connected but unable to access resources, contact IT support for network and DNS checks.

## When to Escalate
Contact IT support if:
- Corporate Wi-Fi remains unavailable.
- Multiple employees experience connectivity problems.
- Wi-Fi disconnects repeatedly.
- Internal applications remain inaccessible.

## Security Precautions
- Connect only to authorized corporate Wi-Fi networks.
- Never share corporate network credentials.
- Avoid unknown or suspicious wireless networks.

## Expected Resolution
The employee successfully connects to corporate Wi-Fi and accesses authorized network resources.
