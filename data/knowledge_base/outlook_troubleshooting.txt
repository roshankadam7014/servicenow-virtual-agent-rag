# Outlook Email Troubleshooting

## Problem Description
An employee experiences problems with Microsoft Outlook, including email synchronization failures, sending or receiving emails, or application performance issues.

## Common Symptoms
- Outlook is not synchronizing emails.
- Unable to send or receive emails.
- Emails remain stuck in the Outbox.
- Outlook displays disconnected status.
- New emails are not appearing.
- Outlook application freezes or crashes.

## Possible Causes
- Internet connectivity problems.
- Outlook is offline.
- Incorrect account configuration.
- Mailbox storage limit reached.
- Corrupted Outlook profile.
- Outdated Outlook application.
- Temporary mail server issues.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Verify that your internet connection is working.
2. Check whether Outlook displays Work Offline mode.
3. If Work Offline is enabled, disable it.
4. Restart the Outlook application.
5. Check whether emails are stuck in the Outbox.
6. Verify that your mailbox has available storage space.
7. Check whether Outlook requires an update.
8. Try accessing corporate email through the organization's approved webmail portal.
9. If synchronization problems continue, contact authorized IT support.

## When to Escalate
Contact IT support if:
- Emails cannot be sent or received.
- Outlook repeatedly disconnects.
- Mailbox synchronization continues to fail.
- Multiple employees experience email issues.
- Outlook crashes repeatedly.

## Security Precautions
- Never share corporate email passwords.
- Do not open suspicious email attachments.
- Report phishing emails through approved company procedures.
- Use only organization-approved email applications.

## Expected Resolution
Outlook synchronizes successfully, and the employee can send and receive corporate emails.
