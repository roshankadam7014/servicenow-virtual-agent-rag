# Laptop Startup Troubleshooting

## Problem Description
An employee's laptop does not start, fails to boot, or gets stuck during startup.

## Common Symptoms
- Laptop does not power on.
- Power button works but screen remains black.
- Laptop gets stuck on the startup logo.
- Operating system fails to load.
- Laptop repeatedly restarts.
- Blue screen or boot error appears.

## Possible Causes
- Battery completely discharged.
- Faulty charger or power adapter.
- Hardware failure.
- Operating system corruption.
- Failed system update.
- External device causing startup problems.
- Insufficient power supply.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Check whether the laptop charger is properly connected.
2. Verify that the power outlet and adapter are working.
3. Connect the charger and allow the battery to charge for some time.
4. Disconnect external USB devices and peripherals.
5. Hold the power button to perform a normal forced shutdown, then try restarting.
6. Check whether the laptop displays any error messages.
7. If the laptop reaches the recovery screen, contact IT support before attempting recovery operations.
8. If the laptop still does not start, contact authorized IT support.

## When to Escalate
Contact IT support if:
- The laptop does not power on after checking the charger.
- The screen remains black.
- The laptop repeatedly restarts.
- Hardware failure is suspected.
- Boot errors continue.

## Security Precautions
- Do not open the laptop or attempt hardware repairs without authorization.
- Do not reinstall the operating system without IT approval.
- Do not remove or format company storage devices.
- Protect company data during troubleshooting.

## Expected Resolution
The laptop starts successfully and loads the operating system. If hardware or operating system problems remain, authorized IT support performs further diagnosis.
